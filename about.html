Who We Are

Trusted Engineering
Partners Across Africa

Zambezi Consulting Engineers (Pty) Ltd (ZCE) was first incorporated in Namibia in 2016 and expanded into Zimbabwe in 2022. ZCE provides electrical and mechanical engineering and consulting services, as well as project management, to the public and private infrastructure sectors. We are registered with the Zimbabwe Association of Consulting Engineers (ZACE), Engineering Council of Zimbabwe (ECZ), Zimbabwe Institution of Engineers (ZIE), National Social Security Authority (NSSA), Procurement Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (PRAZ), and Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA). ZCE has offices in Harare, Bulawayo, and Windhoek, Namibia, and has delivered projects in Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Zambia, and Namibia.

Our team of engineers collectively brings over 50 years of professional experience, having successfully delivered numerous landmark and complex projects across Zimbabwe and the wider SADC region. With extensive expertise in engineering design, construction supervision, contract administration, and project management, our engineers consistently uphold the highest standards of technical excellence and integrity.

All our professionals are duly registered with ZACE, ECZ, and ZIE, among other recognized professional bodies — underscoring our commitment to regulatory compliance, ethical practice, and continuous professional development.

Health, Safety & Environment

We are committed to the health and safety of our employees and the protection of the environment in all business activities. HSE is every employee's responsibility.

Quality Management System

Quality and service excellence are the foundation on which we have built our reputation. Our QMS is certified and drives sustained, profitable growth through client satisfaction.

Responsibility & Sustainability

Social Responsibility
