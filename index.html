Who We Are

Trusted Engineering
Partners Across Africa

Zambezi Consulting Engineers (Pty) Ltd, formed in 2016 in Namibia and incorporated in 2022 in Zimbabwe, provides multi-disciplinary development engineering, consulting services, and project management to the public and private infrastructure sectors in emerging markets. With offices in Windhoek, Katima Mulilo, Harare, and Bulawayo — ZCE is strategically located in areas earmarked for development and upgrading of existing infrastructure across the SADC region.
