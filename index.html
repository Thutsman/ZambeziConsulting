Who We Are

Trusted Engineering
Partners Across Africa

Zambezi Consulting Engineers (Pty) Ltd, formed in 2016 in Namibia and incorporated in 2022 in Zimbabwe, provides multi-disciplinary development engineering, consulting services, and project management to the public and private infrastructure sectors in emerging markets. With offices in Harare and Bulawayo — ZCE is strategically located in areas earmarked for development and upgrading of existing infrastructure across the SADC region.

Health, Safety & Environment

We are committed to the health and safety of our employees and the protection of the environment in all business activities. HSE is every employee's responsibility.

Our People

Engineering Team

Eng. Abel Mashoko

Electrical Engineering Lead

Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering from the University of Zimbabwe. With 20 years of experience in the design and supervision of electrical engineering projects, Eng. He has delivered projects across Zimbabwe, Botswana, Namibia, Mozambique, and Zambia.

  • MV/LV Reticulation
  • Substations
  • Energy Management

Eng. Tatenda Cock

Mechanical Engineering Lead

Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Zimbabwe, Master of Industrial Engineering, and Project Management Professional (PMP®). With 15 years of experience in Zimbabwe, South Africa, and Namibia, Eng. Cock specialises in detailed design, technical specifications, bills of quantities, project management, supervision, and inspections of mechanical systems — including fire protection, water systems, thermal energy storage, VRF and VAV systems, heat recovery, smoke and air extraction, ventilation, and pressurisation.

  • HVAC & Fire Protection
  • VRF & VAV Systems
  • PMP® Certified

Eng. Lovejoy Ndebele

Electronics Engineering Lead

Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Electronics Engineering from the National University of Science and Technology (NUST). With 17 years of experience in detailed design, technical specifications, bills of quantities, project management, supervision, and inspections, Eng. Ndebele leads fire detection, voice evacuation, fire suppression, CCTV, access control, IT, and building management systems. His portfolio includes world-renowned hotels, airports, hospitals, and infrastructure across Zimbabwe, Botswana, Namibia, Mozambique, and Zambia — including Hilton, Radisson Blu, and Holiday Inn properties.
