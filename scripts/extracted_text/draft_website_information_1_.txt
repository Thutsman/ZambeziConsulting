Registered with the Association of Consulting Engineers of Namibia (ACEN) and Green Building Council South Africa (GBCSA), Zambezi Consulting Engineers (Pty) Ltd (ZCE), formed in 2016 in Namibia, provides multi-disciplinary development engineering and consulting services solutions as well as project management to the public and private infrastructure sectors in developing countries.  Our expertise cover the core sectors of Civil, Structures, Water & Sanitation, Transportation, Urban Development, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering and Mining. With offices in Namibia, in Windhoek and Katima Mulilo, in Zimbabwe, in Harare and Bulawayo, ZCE is strategically located in areas that are earmarked by the public and private sectors for further development and upgrading of existing infrastructure. ZCE has done work in Namibia and the SADC region.

EXPERTISE & SERVICES
WATER SUPPLY & SANITATION
We specialize in water and sanitation systems surveys, planning, design and construction supervision and project management services for water and sewage treatment works, upgrade of existing infrastructure, development engineering.
Our involvement encompasses water supply schemes which include the following: Ground water potential studies and Abstraction, Surface Water abstraction and Intake, Water Pumping Stations, Water Treatment Plants, Water Transmission and cross country pipelines, Water Supply Tunnels, Water Distribution and Water Balance Studies, Water Retaining Structures, Rain water Harvesting.
Our experts have been involved in aspects of waste water from property connections to providing recycle and reuse of waste water.
SUSTAINABLE URBAN DEVELOPMENT
We offer comprehensive full service offering extending from structural, civil, mechanical, electrical, plumbing (MEP), fire and life safety (FLS) engineering surveys, planning, design and construction supervision and project management services of new builds and renovations across a very broad and diverse range of building project types including commercial, institutional, government, educational, retail centres, industrial and mining structures i.e. warehouses  and heavy machinery complexes, cold store buildings, pharmaceutical processing and packaging buildings, energy projects – retail fuel stations, healthcare facilities, safety and security properties.
Our services also extend to the development of urban infrastructure for a comprehensive cross-section of new residential areas, alterations, additions and restoration work to existing residential areas, housing schemes, industrial townships, marinas, and leisure resorts.
Our experience encompasses the provision of bulk services and bulk earthworks in preparation of building projects, as well as the provision of roads and infrastructure services surrounding buildings or for new residential townships.
Our expertise also include High/Medium Voltage electrical infrastructure associated with power distribution and reticulation as well as renewable energy engineering.
ZCE provides expertise in solar energy consulting and engineering and technical advisory services and assist clients meet challenges in planning and executing solar photovoltaic energy projects and offering robust, independent solar energy assessments
RENEWABLE SUSTAINABLE TECHNOLOGIES
We offer comprehensive full service offering extending from structural, civil, electrical and electronics engineering surveys, planning, design and construction supervision and project management services in the areas of renewable energy, energy efficiency, grid interconnections, energy storage, off-grid and backup power to public and private clients across the utility scale, industrial, commercial and agro-sectors.
AGRICULTURE ENGINEERING
Our expertise in the agro-sector extends to surveys, planning, design and construction supervision and project management of seed processing plants and associated infrastructure like roads and pavement. Our involvement included market trends and analysis, market segmentation, market break-up by region, plant capacity strategic recommendations, detailed process flow, land, location and site development, plant layout planning, plant machinery, raw materials and packaging, project economics, installation supervision and training and maintenance plan and servicing contracting.
Seed processing plants are a component of the seed industry responsible for upgrading seed, improving planting condition of seed and applying chemical protectants to the seed and they are vital for providing farmers with high quality seed to enhance agricultural productivity and facilitate job creation.
