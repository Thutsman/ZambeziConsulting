INTRODUCTION
Registered with the Association of Consulting Engineers of Namibia (ACEN) and Green Building
Council South Africa (GBCSA), Zambezi Consulting Engineers (Pty) Ltd (ZCE), formed in 2016 in Namibia, and incorporated in 2022 in Zimbabwe, provides multi-disciplinary professional engineering and consulting services solutions as well as project management to the public and private infrastructure sectors in emerging markets.  With offices in Namibia, in Windhoek and Katima Mulilo, in Zimbabwe, in Harare and Bulawayo, ZCE is strategically located in areas that are earmarked by the public and private sectors for further development and upgrading of existing infrastructure. ZCE has done work in Namibia and the SADC region.

Health, Safety & Environmental
ZCE is committed to the health and safety of our employees and the protection of the environment. Effective Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) protection in our business is every employee's responsibility.
It is the intention of Zambezi Consulting Engineers to achieve high standards of Health and Safety and Environment Performance in all aspects of its business activities, consistent with its pursuit of quality and performance generally, and to foster a culture of risk elimination, minimisation and control and protection of the environment throughout the Company.
ZCE management is fully committed to the requirements and the spirit of this HSE plan and to continual improvement of our HSE performance.
Quality Management System
Quality and service excellence are the foundation on which we have built our reputation, and we maintain a policy of quality management. We strive to provide high quality and innovative engineering and development solutions.
It is the policy of ZCE that our services to the client are performed in a manner that satisfies client’s and relevant interested parties needs and expectations, compliance obligations with regard to quality, reliability, competitiveness, health and safety, environment, reliability and performance criteria and in a timely manner while complying with all relevant requirements, legislation and standards.  Our policy is to achieve sustained, profitable growth by providing services which consistently satisfy the needs and expectations of our clients.
This Policy has been formalised and is certified to the Company’s Quality Management System.
